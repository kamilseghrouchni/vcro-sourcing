What you type → what happens

Ask a question. Every answer is cited.

vcro "AD plasma metabolomics cohorts"

5 cohorts ranked by fit, each with N, platform, PI, and evidence quotes

vcro "FFPE tissue proteomics feasibility"

Signal synthesis across 12 papers with replication status

vcro "CSF biomarker access routes"

Access paths, contact PIs, and pricing for 3 biobanks

9 steps from question to recommendation

Not every query needs all 9. A feasibility question stops at step 6.

01Understand

Parse query into structured request

02Search

PubMed + Europe PMC + ClinicalTrials.gov

03Validate

Classify results: relevant, tangential, or noise

04Map & Fetch

Convert PMIDs to PMC IDs, download sections

05Extract

Pull intelligence dimensions from papers in batches

06Signal

Synthesize evidence across papers

07Contacts

PI names, providers, access routes

08Rank

Top 5 cohorts ordered by fit, not raw N

09Deliver

Markdown recommendation with full provenance
