# Depressive Symptoms and Amyloid Pathology

## Abstract

### Question

What is the association between depressive symptoms and amyloid pathology in older individuals without dementia?

### Findings

In this cross-sectional study of 49 centers including 12 769 participants, the presence of depressive symptoms was not consistently associated with amyloid pathology in participants with normal cognition and was associated with a lower frequency of amyloid pathology in participants with mild cognitive impairment.

### Meaning

Mechanisms other than amyloid accumulation may commonly underlie depressive symptomatology in later life.
