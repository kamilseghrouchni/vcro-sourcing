# NEWS: The 2026 Metabolomics publication awards

As the Editor-in-Chief of Metabolomics, this continues to be a personal favourite among our editorial news items

Another incredible year has come and gone. And with it, comes a new group of truly outstanding award winners to celebrate! In 2016 Metabolomics took the decision to applaud our top authors with publication awards, and this tradition showcases the outstanding research we are proud and honoured to publish. This annual event is a joy for the whole editorial team and a wonderful reminder of the privilege we share in highlighting your most impactful research and reviews.

Metabolomics is delighted to recognise excellence across two main award categories, with winners selected based on the highest number of article downloads from our journal in 2025. These include the following:

Best Paper Award is awarded to the Primary Research Article with the highest total number of downloads during the previous year (Volume 21). The first author of the winning paper receives a complimentary ebook from Springer (value 200€). We also highlight the work on the front cover of Metabolomics.The runner-up paper – the first author receives a complimentary ebook from Springer (value 100€).Best Review Award is awarded to the Review Article with the highest total number of downloads also in Volume 21. The first author of the winning paper receives complimentary e-books from Springer (value 200€). Again, we highlight the review on the front cover of Metabolomics.The runner-up paper – the first author receives a complimentary ebook from Springer (value 100€).

To be eligible for these awards, articles published in Metabolomics must fall into one of two categories: Primary Research Papers (including metabolomics standards, best practice articles, short communications, and letters) or Review Articles (including white papers). Eligible articles must have been published in their final form in 2025, which corresponds to Volume 21.A big round of applause for our 2026 winners!!!
