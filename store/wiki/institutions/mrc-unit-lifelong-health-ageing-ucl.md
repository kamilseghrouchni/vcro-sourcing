# MRC Unit for Lifelong Health and Ageing at UCL

## Summary

The MRC Unit for Lifelong Health and Ageing at University College London (UCL) is a distinct MRC unit from the Bristol MRC IEU, though both contributed to the life-course plasma metabolomics study (PMC10873397). Authors affiliated with this unit co-authored the ALSPAC and UK Biobank NMR metabolomics analysis. The unit is not the primary data governance body for either cohort in this study.
