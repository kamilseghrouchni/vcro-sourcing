## Overview

The Second Affiliated Hospital of Xi'an Jiaotong University is a major Chinese teaching hospital in Xi'an, Shaanxi Province — distinct from the First Affiliated Hospital (which hosts the ALS GC/MS cohort). In the vCRO context it is the primary collection site for the SMA nusinersen multi-omic cohort (CSF metabolomics at baseline and 6 months; PMC12488785), with Yang Changhong as lead PI and Li Dan as corresponding author. A secondary collection site at Hanzhong Central Hospital also contributed patients.
