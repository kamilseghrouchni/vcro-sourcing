# University Medicine Greifswald

## Summary

University Medicine Greifswald hosts the NMR core facility (Bruker AVANCE-II 600 MHz with TOPSPIN 3.2) where DELCODE urine samples were measured. The DELCODE data contact, Johannes Hertel, is affiliated with both the University of Galway and DZHK Greifswald. The instrument uses NOESYPRESAT pulse sequence with automated SampleJet delivery and B.I.Quant-UR quantification software. This institution is distinct from the DZNE, which is the data governance body for DELCODE.
