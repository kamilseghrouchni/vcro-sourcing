# Alfred Hospital / Monash University (ASPREE Clinical Coordination and Ethics)

## Summary

Alfred Hospital (Melbourne) and Monash University are co-listed as the institutional ethics anchor and academic lead for the ASPREE (ASPirin in Reducing Events in the Elderly) randomised controlled trial. The Alfred Hospital Ethics Committee issued the ethics approval for ASPREE lipidomics sub-studies (approval #523/21). Monash University, through investigator Paul Lacaze, holds the academic coordination role for ASPREE. These two organisations are combined here as hinted by the source paper; a future enrichment pass may split them into separate entities.
