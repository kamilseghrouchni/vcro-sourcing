## Overview

The First Affiliated Hospital of Xi'an Jiaotong University is a major Chinese teaching hospital in Xi'an, Shaanxi Province. It is distinct from the Second Affiliated Hospital of Xi'an JTU (which ran the SMA nusinersen cohort). In the vCRO context, the First Affiliated Hospital is the collection site for the ALS serum GC/MS metabolomics cohort (n=23 ALS + 25 controls, published 2021; PMC8531355), with Jingxia Dang as senior author and Jia Rui as lead PI.
