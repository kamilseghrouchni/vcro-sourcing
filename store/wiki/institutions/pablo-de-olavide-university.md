# Pablo de Olavide University, Seville

## Summary

Pablo de Olavide University, Seville (Laboratory of Functional Neuroscience, CIBERNED) contributed 74 plasma samples from amyloid-PET confirmed cognitively normal healthy controls to the IRBLleida lipidome study (PMC11095469). This is a separate institution and recruitment arm from the Lleida patient cohort. The PI, José Luís Cantero, led the control sub-cohort collection. Amyloid-PET confirmation of healthy status makes these controls particularly valuable for biomarker studies requiring certified amyloid-negative comparison subjects.

## Sources

- PMC11095469: "Seventy-four plasma samples originated from the Universidad Pablo de Olavide, Sevilla. These controls were part of a cohort of healthy older subjects whose negative amyloid status had been confirmed by Aβ-positron emission tomography (PET) scans."
