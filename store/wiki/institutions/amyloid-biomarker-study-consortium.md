# Amyloid Biomarker Study Consortium

## Summary

The Amyloid Biomarker Study Consortium is a 49-centre international collaboration that harmonised cross-sectional data from older individuals without dementia to study associations between amyloid pathology and clinical features. It produced the largest published pooled analysis in the pre-dementia amyloid space, encompassing 12,769 participants with amyloid status ascertainment (CSF, PET, or plasma-based) and cognitive staging into normal cognition and MCI. The consortium's data governance structure and whether data are accessible through a single portal or site-by-site MTAs is not documented in the captured paper text.
