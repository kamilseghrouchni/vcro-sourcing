# F. Hoffmann-La Roche Ltd / Genentech

## Summary

F. Hoffmann-La Roche Ltd / Genentech is the pharma co-sponsor of the DIAN-TU-001 gantenerumab double-blind trial (NCT01760005) and the open-label extension (NCT06424236). Roche employees are listed as co-authors who contributed to study design, data analysis, and data interpretation. Roche also participates in the broader DIAN-TU Pharma Consortium alongside AbbVie, Biogen, BMS, Eisai, Eli Lilly, Ionis, Janssen, and Prothena. Commercial use rights for DIAN-TU data may be contested due to this co-investigator role.
