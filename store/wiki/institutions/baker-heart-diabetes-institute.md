# Baker Heart and Diabetes Institute

## Summary

Baker Heart and Diabetes Institute (Melbourne, Australia) is the primary lipidomics assay generation site for both the ADNI plasma lipidomics and ASPREE dementia sub-cohort studies. Baker is a member of ADMC and operates a targeted LC-MS/MS lipidomics platform (Agilent 6490 and 6495C, dMRM mode) profiling 781 lipid species across 49 lipid classes. Full platform details are described at metabolomics.baker.edu.au/method. For ADNI, Baker distributes data through the ADMC/Duke channel; for ASPREE, Baker is both the assay generation site and holds the data with access dependent on PI (Peter Meikle) approval.
