# USC Laboratory of Neuro Imaging (LONI) — ADNI Data Coordinating Center

## Summary

The USC Laboratory of Neuro Imaging (LONI) serves as the Data Coordinating Center for the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) and hosts the LONI Image and Data Archive (IDA) at ida.loni.usc.edu. All ADNI data — including the Phase 1 serum lipidomics dataset assayed at UC Davis and the ADNI-1/GO/2 plasma lipidomics dataset generated by Baker Institute — are distributed through this portal under a formal data use agreement. PMC12269576 confirms the same access route under the full formal name "Laboratory of Neuro Imaging Image and Data Archive." Data are also available via the AD Knowledge Portal.
