## Overview

The London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine (LSHTM) is a UK public health research institution. In the vCRO context it provided whole-blood EDTA frozen samples from its biobank for the EPI-ME ME/CFS discovery study (PMC12506310), where Oxford BioDynamics' EpiSwitch 3D chromatin conformation array platform was applied to identify ME/CFS-specific chromatin markers. Oxford BioDynamics is the study sponsor and funded the research in full.
