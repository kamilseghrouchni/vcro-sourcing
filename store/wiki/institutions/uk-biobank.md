# UK Biobank

## Summary

UK Biobank is a large-scale population-based prospective cohort study with ~500,000 participants aged 40–69 at recruitment (2006–2010) from across the UK. It has North West Multi-Centre Research Ethics Committee (MREC) Research Tissue Bank approval. PMC10873397 used the phase-one NMR metabolomics release (n=118,466 non-fasting EDTA plasma, 249 metabolites measured by Nightingale 1H-NMR). PMC12443623 used UK Biobank for BMI PGS tuning (~20,000 EUR) and validation (~340,000 EUR). Summary GWAS results from the IEU-OpenGWAS platform are publicly accessible.
