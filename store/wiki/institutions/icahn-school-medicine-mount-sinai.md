# Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai

## Summary

The Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai (ISMMS) encompasses the Charles Bronfman Institute for Personalized Medicine, which hosts BioMe Biobank — an ancestrally and culturally diverse EHR-linked biorepository with DNA and plasma samples from across the Mount Sinai Health System in New York City. ISMMS also serves as the coordinating IRB and data custodian for the Dartmouth ALS hair cohort study. Senior author Manish Arora (COI: founder/CEO of Linus Biotechnology Inc.) is based here. PMC12443623 used BioMe for PGS validation; PMC12444149 used Mount Sinai as the IRB and data hub for the ALS hair elemental study.

## Sources

- PMC12443623: "The Institute for Personalized Medicine BioMe Biobank, founded in 2007, is an ancestrally and culturally highly diverse EHR-linked biorepository enrolling participants non-selectively from across the Mount Sinai Health System in New York City."
