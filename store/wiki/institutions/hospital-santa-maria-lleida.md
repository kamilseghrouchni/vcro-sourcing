# Hospital Universitari Santa Maria de Lleida

## Summary

Hospital Universitari Santa Maria de Lleida hosted the Cognitive Disorders Unit that prospectively recruited MCI and AD patients from June 2014 to December 2016 for the lipidome cohort study. Biospecimens are stored at IRBLleida Biobank (B.0000682), which is the formal access point. Clinical leads are Gerard Piñol-Ripoll and Farida Dakterzada. The hospital is distinct from Pablo de Olavide University, which contributed the healthy control sub-cohort.

## Sources

- PMC11095469: "Patients with amnestic MCI and AD were recruited prospectively and consecutively from a sample of outpatients who visited the Cognitive Disorders Unit of the Hospital Universitari Santa Maria de Lleida (Lleida, Spain) from June 2014 to December 2016."
