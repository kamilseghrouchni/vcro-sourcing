## Overview

Oxford BioDynamics Plc (OBD) is a UK-listed life sciences company that developed the EpiSwitch® platform for epigenomic 3D chromatin conformation profiling. In the vCRO context it is the sole funder, data rights holder, and commercial platform developer for the EPI-ME ME/CFS study (PMC12506310). EpiSwitch identifies chromosomal conformation changes as disease-specific biomarkers in peripheral blood; the EPI-ME study achieved a 3-marker AUC of 0.99 for severe ME/CFS in an independent validation cohort.
