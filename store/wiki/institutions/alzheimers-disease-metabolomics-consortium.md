# Alzheimer's Disease Metabolomics Consortium (ADMC)

## Summary

The Alzheimer's Disease Metabolomics Consortium (ADMC) is the consortium-level sponsor that generates and distributes metabolomics data for the ADNI study. Based at Duke University under the direction of Rima Kaddurah-Daouk, ADMC coordinates metabolomics data generation across multiple platforms including the Baker Heart and Diabetes Institute lipidomics pipeline. ADMC data are accessible via the LONI portal (ida.loni.usc.edu) and the AD Knowledge Portal, though the lipidomics data layer may require a separate request to the ADMC/Duke University team. PMC12269576 adds the Duke University host affiliation; the underlying entity is identical to the ADMC referenced in PMC10103184.
