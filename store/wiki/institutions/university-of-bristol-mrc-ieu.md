# University of Bristol — MRC Integrative Epidemiology Unit

## Summary

The MRC Integrative Epidemiology Unit at the University of Bristol is the lead PI institution for ALSPAC NMR metabolomics studies, with Joshua Bell and Emma Anderson as the primary investigators. The unit also hosts the IEU-OpenGWAS platform, which distributes publicly accessible GWAS summary statistics including NMR metabolomics GWAS results from UK Biobank (accessible at gwas.mrcieu.ac.uk/datasets/?gwas_id__icontains=met-d). Individual-level ALSPAC data are available via managed access at www.bristol.ac.uk/alspac/researchers/access. PMC12443623 also uses University of Bristol as the data manager for ALSPAC in the BMI PGS life-course study.
