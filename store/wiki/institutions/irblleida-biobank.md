# IRBLleida Biobank

## Summary

IRBLleida Biobank (registry number B.0000682) is a Spanish public academic biobank affiliated with the Institut de Recerca Biomèdica de Lleida and registered under PLATAFORMA BIOBANCOS (PT17/0015/0027). It holds EDTA plasma, CSF, and buffy coat DNA samples from the Hospital Universitari Santa Maria Lleida AD/MCI cohort (n=289, collected 2014–2016). No industry conflicts of interest are declared by the PIs, but commercial use must be explicitly confirmed. Aliquot depletion status is unknown given at least one prior GC-FID assay run.
