## Overview

Royal Cornwall Hospitals NHS Trust is an NHS foundation trust in Cornwall, UK. In the vCRO context it is the collection site for the EPI-ME ME/CFS independent validation cohort (n=24 ME/CFS patients + n=45 controls, PMC12506310). Oxford BioDynamics (the study funder) holds the commercial rights; Royal Cornwall provided the samples under a collaboration agreement.
