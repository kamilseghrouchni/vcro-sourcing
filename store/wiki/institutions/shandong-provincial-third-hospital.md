# Shandong Provincial Third Hospital, Department of Neurology

## Summary

Shandong Provincial Third Hospital (Department of Neurology, Jinan, China) is the collection site for the MTBLS8662 urine metabolomics cohort. The cohort enrolled 162 participants aged 50+ (57 AD, 43 MCI, 62 CN) and urine samples were processed for LC-MS/MS untargeted metabolomics. The institution is implied to be predominantly serving a Han Chinese population. Raw metabolomics data are publicly deposited in MetaboLights under accession MTBLS8662.
