# US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention / ATSDR

## Summary

The CDC and its Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry (ATSDR) jointly sponsor and maintain the CDC National ALS Biorepository, which holds hair samples from ALS-positive patients collected across the United States. The biorepository is a nationwide resource with broader ethnic diversity than regional clinical biobanks (18.8% non-White). Funding for associated research is through NIH/CDC HHEAR consortium grants. Data and material access terms are governed by CDC/ATSDR policy and have not been published in the captured paper text.
