# Deutsches Zentrum für Neurodegenerative Erkrankungen (DZNE)

## Summary

DZNE is the German national research centre for neurodegenerative diseases and the lead data governance institution for the DELCODE multicentre observational study on pre-dementia Alzheimer's disease. The DELCODE cohort (n=177 urine NMR metabolomics sub-cohort) is administered and funded by DZNE, with data available on direct researcher request. NIA grants (U19AG063744 AGMP, R01AG046171, and others) co-fund the project. The DELCODE data contact is Johannes Hertel at University Medicine Greifswald.
