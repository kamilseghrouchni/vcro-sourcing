## Overview

Chang Gung Memorial Hospital (CGMH) is one of Taiwan's largest medical systems, with its principal campus in Linkou. The ALS Neurology Clinic at CGMH recruited and characterised a plasma metabolomics cohort (Biocrates AbsoluteIDQ p180) over December 2018 – November 2020, publishing the SVM classification model in 2021 (PMC8699018). The metabolomics analysis was performed at the Metabolomics Core Laboratory at Chang Gung University.
