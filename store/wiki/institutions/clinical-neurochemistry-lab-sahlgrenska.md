## Overview

The Clinical Neurochemistry Laboratory (CNL) at Sahlgrenska University Hospital, University of Gothenburg is one of the world's foremost neuro-biomarker laboratories. Under Johan Gobom and Henrik Zetterberg, it served as the collection and sample-processing site for the Target ALS longitudinal biofluid proteomics cohort published in 2025 (PMC12927225). The CNL also processes a broader CSF surplus from the Sahlgrenska neurological clinic, which may represent an independent access opportunity beyond the Target ALS cohort.

## Notes

CNL Sahlgrenska is a WHO collaborating centre for biomarker standardisation. Data generated through the Target ALS programme flows through the Target ALS data engine; contact targetals.org for the formal data access application.
