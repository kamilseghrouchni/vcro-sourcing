## Overview

Akershus University Hospital (AHUS) is a Norwegian tertiary hospital located in Lørenskog, affiliated with the University of Oslo. In the vCRO context it appears as a processing and collection site for Netherlands Brain Bank (NBB) ALS postmortem spinal cord material (PMC12603167), co-led by Evandro F. Fang's group in collaboration with Huanxing Su's lab at the University of Macau.

## Notes

Akershus is not an independent biorepository; it receives and processes NBB-consented tissue. Any access request must go through the NBB portal (www.e-nbb.org) first, with Akershus listed as the local site.
