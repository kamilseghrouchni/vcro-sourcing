# Department of Neurology, University of Michigan

## Summary

The Department of Neurology at the University of Michigan (Ann Arbor, MI) serves as the primary clinical collection site for the ALS gut microbiome and plasma metabolomics cohort. ALS patients and controls were recruited and biospecimens collected at this department. It is distinct from the NeuroNetwork for Emerging Therapies — another named research unit at UMich that acts as the cohort sponsor — and from the University of North Dakota, which provided bioinformatics analysis.
