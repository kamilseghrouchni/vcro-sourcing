# Dartmouth Health / Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Centre

## Summary

Dartmouth Health (including Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Centre) is the primary clinical recruitment and collection site for the Dartmouth ALS Biobank hair cohort. Patients with positive and negative ALS diagnoses were recruited from the Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Centre and the Dartmouth Health Department of Neurology. Senior author Elijah W. Stommel is the key contact at this institution. IRB approval was granted by Mount Sinai, Columbia University, and Dartmouth University jointly.
