## Overview

The Leiden Academic Centre for Drug Research (LACDR) at Leiden University hosts the Metabolomics and Analytics Centre under Thomas Hankemeier, one of Europe's leading metabolomics groups. LACDR provides analytical services to multiple cohorts: it processed the Geneva COSCODE plasma/fecal samples using SCIEX 6600+ HILIC polar metabolomics (PMC12572818) and the ADNI 2/GO plasma samples using the UHPLC-MS/MS oxylipin-endocannabinoid panel (~260 analytes; PMC12857118). LACDR does not independently hold biobank or cohort access rights.
