# BioIVT

## Summary

BioIVT is a commercial biospecimen company from which de-identified plasma samples were sourced for the TJU sncRNA study (PMC10933579). Both Mtb-positive and healthy matched donor plasma aliquots were purchased; the original collection site, country of origin, and donor recruitment pathway are not documented in the paper. Physical samples require a commercial order from BioIVT; pricing and lot availability must be confirmed directly with the vendor.
