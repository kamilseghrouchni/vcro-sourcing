# IRCCS Ospedale Policlinico San Martino

## Summary

IRCCS Ospedale Policlinico San Martino (Genova, Italy) operates both an SPF animal facility and an on-site GMP radiopharmacy capable of synthesising [18F]DPA-714 for micro-PET studies. The institution hosted the SOD1G93A transgenic mouse ALS model cohort with three longitudinal imaging timepoints (days 60, 90, 120). FFPE quadriceps muscle blocks and fresh-frozen brain/spleen homogenates are held at this institution. Access requires direct contact with the corresponding author.
