## Overview

Helmholtz Zentrum München (HMGU) is Germany's primary research centre for environmental health, affiliated with the Technical University of Munich. In the vCRO context, HMGU is the home institution of Gabi Kastenmüller and Matthias Arnold, both of whom are senior contributors to the Alzheimer's Disease Metabolomics Consortium (ADMC) and the ADNI longitudinal serum metabolomics programme. HMGU provides bioinformatics and statistical metabolomics capability to the ADMC rather than acting as an independent sample custodian.
