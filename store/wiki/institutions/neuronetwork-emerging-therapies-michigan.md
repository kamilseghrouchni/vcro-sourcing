# NeuroNetwork for Emerging Therapies, University of Michigan

## Summary

NeuroNetwork for Emerging Therapies is a named research unit within the University of Michigan that serves as the sponsor for the ALS gut microbiome and plasma metabolomics cohort. Multiple lead investigators including Eva L. Feldman, Stephen A. Goutman, Kai Guo, and Claudia Figueroa-Romero list NeuroNetwork as a co-affiliation alongside the Department of Neurology. This unit is distinct from the Department of Neurology (which serves as the collection_site) and from the University of North Dakota (which provides bioinformatics data analysis).
