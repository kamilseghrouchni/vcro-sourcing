## Overview

The Institute of Chinese Medical Sciences (ICMS) at the University of Macau is a research institute focused on pharmacology and biomedical sciences. In the vCRO context it is the lead PI institution for Huanxing Su's group, which co-led the NBB ALS spinal cord / WiCell iPSC autophagy study (PMC12603167) with Evandro Fang's group at Akershus/Oslo. ICMS Macau does not hold an independent biorepository; sample access routes through NBB and WiCell respectively.
