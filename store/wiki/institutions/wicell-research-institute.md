## Overview

WiCell Research Institute Inc. is a non-profit stem cell repository affiliated with the University of Wisconsin, providing iPSC and hESC lines to researchers worldwide. In the vCRO context it supplied three familial ALS patient iPSC lines (C9orf72, TDP-43, SOD1 genotypes) to the Huanxing Su/Evandro Fang study (PMC12603167). WiCell operates a commercial catalogue with standard material transfer agreements for research applications; therapeutic commercial use requires a separate licence.
