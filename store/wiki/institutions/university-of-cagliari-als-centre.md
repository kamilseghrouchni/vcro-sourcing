## Overview

The University of Cagliari ALS Referral Centre is a specialised Italian neurology service serving the Sardinian island population, which has one of the world's highest ALS prevalence rates. In the vCRO context it recruited the Sardinian ALS case-control cohort (n=12 ALS + n=8 controls) for a serum TMAO metabolomics study using the Waters Acquity UPLC + TQD HILIC platform (PMC12907680). Salvatore Sotgia is the lead PI and corresponding author.
