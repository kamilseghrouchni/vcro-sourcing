# University of Western Ontario (Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry)

## Summary

The University of Western Ontario (also known as Western University), through its Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry (London, Ontario, Canada), is the home institution of Taylor W. Schmitz (senior/corresponding author) and Hayley R. C. Shanks (lead author) for the ADNI Phase 1 serum lipidomics study published in PMC10103184. The Schulich name is preserved in the canonical name and aliases for disambiguation; the slug anchors at university level for future-proofing. Associated research units include Lawson Health Research Institute and Robarts Research Institute.
