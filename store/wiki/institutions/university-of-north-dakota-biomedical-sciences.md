# Department of Biomedical Sciences, University of North Dakota

## Summary

The Department of Biomedical Sciences at the University of North Dakota (Grand Forks, ND) acts as the data provider for computational and bioinformatics analysis of the University of Michigan ALS gut microbiome and plasma metabolomics cohort. Lead computational analyst Junguk Hur is based here. This institution's role is distinct from the University of Michigan Neurology (collection_site) and NeuroNetwork for Emerging Therapies (sponsor).
