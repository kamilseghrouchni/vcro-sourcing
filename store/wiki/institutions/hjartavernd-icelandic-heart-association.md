## Overview

Hjartavernd (the Icelandic Heart Association) is the non-governmental organisation that sponsors and operates the AGES-Reykjavik Study (AGES-RS), a longitudinal population-based cohort of aging in Iceland. In the vCRO context it appears as the sponsor of the AGES-RS serum metabolomics dataset used as an independent replication cohort for preclinical AD serum metabolomics (PMC4947451). The NIA and the National Institutes of Health are co-funders of AGES-RS, providing an additional layer of public-funding-based data-sharing pressure.
