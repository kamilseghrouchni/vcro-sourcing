## Overview

Majorbio Biomedical Technology Ltd. is a Shanghai-based commercial multi-omics service provider. In the vCRO context it performed untargeted UHPLC-Q Exactive LC-MS/MS metabolomics on CSF samples from the Xi'an Jiaotong University Second Affiliated Hospital SMA nusinersen cohort (PMC12488785). Majorbio acts as a contracted service provider; data ownership resides with the commissioning institution.
