# Dominantly Inherited Alzheimer Network Trials Unit (DIAN-TU), Washington University in St. Louis

## Summary

The DIAN-TU at Washington University in St. Louis is the coordinating centre for the Dominantly Inherited Alzheimer Network trial (DIAN-TU-001, NCT01760005 and NCT06424236) and the DIAN Observational Study (NCT00869817). Director Randall J. Bateman is the lead PI. DIAN-TU data access is governed by a published policy (dian.wustl.edu) requiring a data/material sharing agreement. The DIAN-TU Pharma Consortium includes F. Hoffmann-La Roche and other major pharma companies as co-funders and co-authors. NIH/NIA grant U01AG042791 is the primary federal funding source.
