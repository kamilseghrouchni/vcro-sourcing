# Uganda Virus Research Institute (UVRI)

## Summary

The Uganda Virus Research Institute (UVRI) co-established the Uganda General Population Cohort (GPC-UGR) in 1989 in collaboration with the Medical Research Council (UK) to monitor the HIV epidemic in rural southwestern Uganda (Kyamulibwa subcounty, Kalungu district). UVRI is the primary collection site for GPC-UGR genomic data and serves as the institutional anchor for this continental African cohort. The cohort was used in PMC12443623 as a representative continental African ancestry group for BMI PGS performance evaluation.
