## Overview

The Indiana University Alzheimer's Disease Research Center (IU ADRC) at Indiana University School of Medicine is one of the NIA-designated ADRCs. In the vCRO context it serves as the analytical home for Andrew Saykin and Shannon Risacher, who contribute to multiple ADNI studies including the multi-phase CSF/plasma biomarker programme (PMC12795606). The IU ADRC maintains its own participant registry with neuroimaging, genetics, and cognitive data that is partially distinct from ADNI.
