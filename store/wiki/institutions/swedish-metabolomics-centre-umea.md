## Overview

The Swedish Metabolomics Centre (SMC) at Umeå University is a national analytical infrastructure facility operating under the SciLifeLab umbrella. It provides untargeted GC-MS and LC-MS metabolomics services to academic and commercial clients. In the vCRO context it performed the untargeted plasma metabolomics analysis for the Karolinska FAD PSEN1-H163Y kindred study (PMC7951103). SMC is a service provider and does not hold data rights or maintain an independent biorepository.
