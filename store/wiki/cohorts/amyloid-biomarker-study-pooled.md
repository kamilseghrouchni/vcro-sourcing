# Amyloid Biomarker Study Pooled Cohort (49 Centers)

## Summary

The Amyloid Biomarker Study pooled cohort harmonises data from 49 international centres, yielding 12,769 participants without dementia (normal cognition and MCI sub-groups). It is a cross-sectional analysis focused on the association between depressive symptoms and amyloid pathology. At this scale, the cohort is well-powered for subgroup analyses including MCI conversion strata and APOE stratification. However, biospecimen type used to ascertain amyloid status (CSF, PET, or plasma) is not confirmed in the captured abstract.

## Real numbers

> "cross-sectional study of 49 centers including 12 769 participants"

[ref: PMC11883504]

At 12,769 participants this is one of the largest pooled amyloid biomarker datasets in the pre-dementia space, which means for the buyer's project that subgroup analyses (e.g., MCI converters, APOE strata) are likely powered even after filtering.

## Multi-site recruitment

> "cross-sectional study of 49 centers including 12 769 participants"

[ref: PMC11883504]

49-site recruitment broadens geographic and institutional diversity, which means for the buyer's project that findings are less susceptible to single-site artefacts but protocol harmonisation across sites will need to be verified before treating samples as interchangeable.

## Eligibility criteria

> "the association between depressive symptoms and amyloid pathology in older individuals without dementia"

[ref: PMC11883504]

The cohort explicitly excludes dementia cases, which means for the buyer's project that it is ideal for pre-symptomatic or prodromal amyloid research but cannot support studies requiring dementia-stage participants.

## Clinical endpoints and scales

> "the presence of depressive symptoms was not consistently associated with amyloid pathology in participants with normal cognition and was associated with a lower frequency of amyloid pathology in participants with mild cognitive impairment"

[ref: PMC11883504]

Amyloid positivity and cognitive staging are captured at scale, which means for the buyer's project that this cohort can support biomarker-stratified analyses linking psychiatric symptoms to AD pathology trajectory without needing an external amyloid readout.

## Negative results

> "the presence of depressive symptoms was not consistently associated with amyloid pathology in participants with normal cognition and was associated with a lower frequency of amyloid pathology in participants with mild cognitive impairment"

[ref: PMC11883504]

The null or inverse association between depression and amyloid load is a published negative result at n=12,769, which means for the buyer's project that designing a study predicated on depression as a proxy for amyloid burden in pre-dementia populations is not supported by this dataset.

## Longitudinal structure

> "In this cross-sectional study of 49 centers including 12 769 participants"

[ref: PMC11883504]

Cross-sectional design means no conversion or trajectory data is available within this analysis, which means for the buyer's project that causal or predictive modelling of amyloid accumulation over time would require linking to a separate longitudinal arm or a different dataset.
