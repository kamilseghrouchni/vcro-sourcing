## Overview

The Sardinian ALS Case-Control Cohort is a single-site cross-sectional exploratory study conducted at the tertiary ALS referral centre of the University of Cagliari (Sardinia, Italy). 12 ALS patients (revised El Escorial criteria; King's stages 1–3; 5 TARDBP, 2 C9orf72, 5 mutation-negative) and 8 healthy controls from the Sulcis-Iglesiente area of southwestern Sardinia were enrolled December 2023 to April 2024. Serum TMAO panel was measured by validated LC-MS/MS (Waters Acquity UPLC + TQD, HILIC, Zorbax RR HILIC column) using d9-TMAO internal standard; method R²>0.995, intra-RSD 2.88%, inter-RSD 4.23%, recovery 95–101% (PMC12907680).

**N:** 12 ALS (5F/7M, median age 59 yr) + 8 controls (4F/4M, median age 61 yr).
**Biospecimen:** Morning fasting serum; serum separator tubes; two-step centrifugation (1900g/10min + 3000g/15min); 250 µL aliquots at −80°C; one thaw for assay.
**Platform:** Waters Acquity UPLC + TQD tandem quadrupole (HILIC mode); 7-analyte panel (TMAO, TML, choline, carnitine, betaine, DMG, gBB, ergothioneine).
**Analytes significant:** TMAO (P=0.0003) and trimethyllysine TML (P=0.002) elevated in ALS.
**Access:** No public portal; contact Sotgia/Borghero at University of Cagliari; ethics approval 28/2024.

## Key Findings (PMC12907680)

- TMAO and TML significantly elevated in ALS vs controls; 5 other trimethylammonium compounds non-significant.
- ALCAR supplementation confounds carnitine-related analytes in 11/12 ALS patients.
- Sardinian founder population (genetic isolate) reduces population stratification noise but limits generalisability.
- Directional discrepancy with Chinese ALS cohort (n=160): TMAO decreased in Chinese patients — cross-cohort inconsistency unresolved.

## Cohort-Level Flags

- Critical confounder: ALCAR supplementation in 11/12 patients elevates carnitine-derived trimethylammonium compounds including TMAO; results not interpretable as disease biomarkers without ALCAR-naive replication.
- Exploratory sample size (N=12 cases): no statistical power for subgroup analysis; all effect sizes hypothesis-generating only.
- Cross-sectional design: no repeated sampling or longitudinal trajectory data available.
- Consent scope for commercial use unconfirmed; direct PI inquiry required.
