## Overview

The ADNI-1 Baseline Serum Metabolomics Cohort (Biocrates p180) is the ADNI-1 wave-specific targeted metabolomics layer generated by the Alzheimer's Disease Metabolomics Consortium (ADMC) at Duke University. Blood was collected at 8 AM after overnight fast, placed immediately on dry ice, and shipped same-day to the ADNI Biomarker Core at the University of Pennsylvania. Metabolomics used the Biocrates AbsoluteIDQ p180 platform (187 metabolites: amino acids, acylcarnitines, sphingomyelins, phosphatidylcholines, hexoses, biogenic amines) on AB SCIEX 4000 QTrap. This cohort served as the validation arm for the sphingolipid-AD progression hypothesis derived from the BLSA serum cohort (PMC5784884).

**N:** 767 baseline serum samples (216 CN, 366 MCI, 185 AD); CSF biomarker sub-set: 403 (109 CN, 186 MCI, 100 AD); all samples passed Met-So QC (zero exclusions); mean storage ~8.7–8.9 years.
**Biospecimen:** Serum; fasting; same-day dry-ice shipment to UPenn; Biocrates p180; 187 metabolites; LOD/2 imputation for sub-LOD values.
**Co-modalities:** Structural MRI (SPARE-AD brain atrophy index) in all 767; CSF t-tau, p-tau, Aβ1-42 (Luminex xMAP / Innogenetics INNO-BIA AlzBio3) in 403; APOE genotype.
**Access:** LONI portal under standard ADNI DUA; ADMC deposited; commercial-use verification required.

## Key Findings (PMC5784884)

- ADNI-1 confirms BLSA sphingolipid finding: higher serum sphingomyelins (SM C16:0, SM C16:1, SM(OH) C14:1, SM C18:1) associated with cognitive decline and AD biomarker load.
- Concurrent CSF/MRI layers allow cross-validation of blood sphingolipid signal against gold-standard ATN markers.
- Zero Met-So exclusions and shorter storage (8.7–8.9 yr vs 13–18 yr in BLSA) make ADNI-1 the higher quality serum substrate.
- Pre-analytical protocol (same-day dry-ice shipment, centralized processing) is robust and reproducible.

## Cohort-Level Flags

- MCI arm (n=366) is the largest diagnostic group and primary resource for MCI-to-AD converter analysis; converter N not specified in this paper for ADNI-1.
- CSF is baseline-only (403-participant subset) — longitudinal CSF-metabolite correlation not supported.
- Lipid-modifying drug adjustment not explicitly documented in this paper — cross-check ADNI medication tables before interpreting sphingolipid signals.
- Predominantly Caucasian, healthy older adults (standard ADNI composition) — limited generalisability to diverse populations.
