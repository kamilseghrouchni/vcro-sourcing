## Overview

The Chang Gung Memorial Hospital ALS Plasma Metabolomics Cohort enrolled 36 ALS patients (revised El Escorial criteria) and 36 age/sex-matched healthy controls at Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, Linkou Branch, Taiwan between 2018 and 2020. Plasma metabolomics was performed using the Biocrates AbsoluteIDQ p180 kit (targeted 187-analyte panel: amino acids, acylcarnitines, sphingomyelins, phosphatidylcholines, hexoses, biogenic amines). Samples collected in EDTA tubes; immediate plasma separation and −80°C storage (PMC8699018).

**N:** 36 ALS (both spinal and bulbar onset) + 36 age/sex-matched controls; median age ~62 yr; median ALSFRS-R 41.
**Biospecimen:** EDTA plasma; immediate processing and −80°C storage; Biocrates p180 platform.
**Eligibility:** ALS: revised El Escorial criteria; King's staging 1–3; excluded: concurrent neurodegenerative, metabolic, or liver disease; active infection; recent major surgery.
**Disease characteristics:** 78% on riluzole; median disease duration ~24 months; 72% spinal onset.
**Access:** No public portal; contact Hung-Chou Kuo at Chang Gung Memorial Hospital; IRB approval documented.

## Key Findings (PMC8699018)

- Significant reductions in specific phosphatidylcholines and sphingomyelins in ALS plasma vs controls.
- Several acylcarnitine species altered, suggesting mitochondrial fatty acid oxidation disruption.
- Lipid alterations did not correlate significantly with ALSFRS-R score — cross-sectional severity-independence finding.
- No external replication cohort included.

## Cohort-Level Flags

- Riluzole confounding (78% of ALS patients): this drug affects multiple metabolic pathways; metabolomics signals may reflect drug effects rather than disease biology.
- Cross-sectional design: no pre-post treatment or longitudinal follow-up.
- Plasma vs serum matrix note: Biocrates p180 runs in both matrices but absolute concentration reference ranges differ; platform-bridging study required before direct comparison with serum-based ADNI/BLSA p180 data.
- Taiwan-specific ancestry and clinical practices may limit generalisability to European or North American ALS cohorts.
- Small matched cohort: adequate for discovery but not for biomarker validation or multivariate subgroup analysis.
