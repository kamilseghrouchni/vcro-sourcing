# Kai Guo

## Summary

Kai Guo holds a lead PI role at the Department of Neurology and the NeuroNetwork for Emerging Therapies at the University of Michigan. He is a co-author on the ALS gut microbiome and plasma metabolomics study (PMC10834248) that longitudinally profiled gut microbiome changes in ALS and correlated them with the plasma metabolome.
