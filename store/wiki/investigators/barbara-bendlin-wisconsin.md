# Barbara B. Bendlin

## Summary

Barbara B. Bendlin is a senior author affiliated with the Wisconsin Alzheimer's Disease Research Center (WADRC) at the University of Wisconsin, Madison. She is the named data access contact for the WADRC/WRAP stool metagenomics sub-cohort (PMC10937638). Her role in the study was as a senior author and co-lead for the Wisconsin cohort data.
