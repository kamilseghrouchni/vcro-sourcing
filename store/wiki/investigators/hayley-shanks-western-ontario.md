# Hayley R. C. Shanks

## Summary

Hayley R. C. Shanks is the lead author on the ADNI Phase 1 serum lipidomics study (PMC10103184), affiliated with the Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry at the University of Western Ontario. Her work investigated how baseline serum unsaturated phosphatidylcholines predict longitudinal basal forebrain degeneration in Alzheimer's disease using the ADNI Phase 1 cohort (n=313 for lipidomics, n=223 with neuroimaging).
