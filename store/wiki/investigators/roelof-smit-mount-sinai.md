# Roelof A. J. Smit

## Summary

Roelof A. J. Smit is the lead PI for the multi-ancestry BMI polygenic score life-course study (PMC12443623). His primary affiliation is with the Charles Bronfman Institute for Personalized Medicine, Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, with additional affiliations at Leiden University Medical Center and the Novo Nordisk Foundation Center for Basic Metabolic Research, University of Copenhagen. He led the PGS derivation and multi-cohort validation analysis.
