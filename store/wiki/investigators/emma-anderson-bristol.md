# Emma L. Anderson

## Summary

Emma L. Anderson is a senior author on the ALSPAC life-course plasma NMR metabolomics study (PMC10873397), affiliated with Bristol Medical School (Population Health Sciences, University of Bristol) and the Division of Psychiatry, UCL. Her role was as senior author overseeing the genetic epidemiology analysis of AD liability and plasma metabolites.
