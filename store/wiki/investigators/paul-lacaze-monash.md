# Paul Lacaze

## Summary

Paul Lacaze is the lead PI for ASPREE (ASPirin in Reducing Events in the Elderly) at Monash University. He is the corresponding author for ASPREE lipidomics data access, which is available upon reasonable request to corresponding authors — no formal portal exists. The ASPREE dementia case-cohort lipidomics subset (n=3495, 402 incident dementia cases, 6.5-year follow-up) is held at Baker Heart and Diabetes Institute but accessed through Lacaze's team at Monash.
