# Isidore Rigoutsos

## Summary

Isidore Rigoutsos is a senior author and PI of the Computational Medicine Center at Thomas Jefferson University (Philadelphia, PA). He is the developer of the isoMiRmap and MINTmap bioinformatics tools used for sncRNA profiling and led the re-analysis of GEO datasets GSE168714 (ALS plasma) and GSE148097 (ALS serum) (PMC12208959). His group also collaborates with the Weinberg ALS Center at TJU on ALS sncRNA biomarker discovery.
