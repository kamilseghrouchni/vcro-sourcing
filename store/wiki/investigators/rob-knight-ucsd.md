# Rob Knight

## Summary

Rob Knight is a senior author affiliated with the Center for Microbiome Innovation at the University of California San Diego. He contributed to the WADRC/WRAP stool metagenomics processing and analysis (PMC10937638), with his lab providing the Qiita default metagenomics workflow and Woltka/Web of Life pipeline for gOTU generation. He is a co-investigator on the Wisconsin stool metagenomics sub-cohort.
