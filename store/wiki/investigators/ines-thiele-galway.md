# Ines Thiele

## Summary

Ines Thiele is the lead PI for the whole-body metabolic modelling analysis linking DELCODE urine NMR metabolomics and Wisconsin WADRC/WRAP stool metagenomics (PMC10937638), affiliated with the School of Medicine at the University of Galway, Ireland. She leads the computational biology component (ERC Horizon 2020 grant 757922). Data access for the DELCODE cohort routes through Johannes Hertel rather than through Thiele directly.
