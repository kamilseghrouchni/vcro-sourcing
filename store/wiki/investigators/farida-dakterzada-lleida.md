# Farida Dakterzada

## Summary

Farida Dakterzada is the corresponding author for the Lleida AD/MCI lipidome study (PMC11095469), affiliated with the Cognitive Disorders Unit at IRBLleida Hospital Universitari Santa Maria, Lleida, Spain. She is a co-investigator on the cohort alongside lead PI Gerard Piñol-Ripoll. She is the primary contact for data and biospecimen queries.
