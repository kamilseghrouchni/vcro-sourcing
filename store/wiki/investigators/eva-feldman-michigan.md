# Eva L. Feldman

## Summary

Eva L. Feldman is the corresponding author and lead PI for the ALS gut microbiome and plasma metabolomics cohort study at the University of Michigan. Affiliated with both the Department of Neurology and the NeuroNetwork for Emerging Therapies at UMich, she is the primary contact for data access inquiries. The cohort study longitudinally profiled gut microbiome changes in ALS and correlated them with the plasma metabolome. NIH and CDC funding sources with data-sharing obligations suggest a formal data access mechanism may be available.
