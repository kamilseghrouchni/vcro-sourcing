# Wang Tingting

## Summary

Wang Tingting is the first author and lead statistical analyst on the ADNI-1/GO/2 longitudinal plasma lipidomics study (PMC12269576), based at Baker Heart and Diabetes Institute. She led the development of the lipidome trajectory model for AD risk prediction, achieving AUC 0.84 for AD vs CN discrimination and AUC 0.70 for MCI-to-AD conversion prediction at baseline. The name is written in East Asian order (family name first) as presented in the source paper.
