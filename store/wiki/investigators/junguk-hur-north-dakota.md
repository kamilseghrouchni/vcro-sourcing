# Junguk Hur

## Summary

Junguk Hur is the lead PI for computational and bioinformatics analyses at the Department of Biomedical Sciences, University of North Dakota (Grand Forks, ND). He contributed the bioinformatics analysis (microbe-metabolite network module construction, Mendelian randomization analysis) for the University of Michigan ALS gut microbiome and plasma metabolomics study (PMC10834248).
