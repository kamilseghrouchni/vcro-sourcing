# Yohei Kirino

## Summary

Yohei Kirino is the lead PI for the BioIVT plasma sncRNA tuberculosis study (PMC10933579) at Thomas Jefferson University. His lab developed and validated the T4 polynucleotide kinase (T4 PNK) pre-treatment sncRNA-seq protocol at the MetaOmics Core Facility. His NIH grants (GM106047, HL150560, AI151641, AI168975, AI171366) and an ACS Research Scholar Grant funded the work. He is the appropriate contact for questions about the BioIVT plasma material re-order or the sncRNA-seq protocol.
