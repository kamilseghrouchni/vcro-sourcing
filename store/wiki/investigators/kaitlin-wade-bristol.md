# Kaitlin H. Wade

## Summary

Kaitlin H. Wade is a co-investigator affiliated with the MRC Integrative Epidemiology Unit, University of Bristol. She contributed to the multi-ancestry BMI polygenic score life-course study (PMC12443623), providing expertise in ALSPAC and UK Biobank Mendelian randomisation and genetic epidemiology approaches.
