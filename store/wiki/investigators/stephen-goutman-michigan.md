# Stephen A. Goutman

## Summary

Stephen A. Goutman is a senior author at the Department of Neurology and the NeuroNetwork for Emerging Therapies at the University of Michigan. He co-leads the ALS gut microbiome and plasma metabolomics cohort study with Eva Feldman (corresponding author), contributing clinical and ALS disease expertise. The study longitudinally profiled gut microbiome changes in ALS and correlated them with the plasma metabolome (PMC10834248).
