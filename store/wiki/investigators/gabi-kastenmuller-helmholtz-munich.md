# Gabi Kastenmüller

## Summary

Gabi Kastenmüller is a senior author for bioinformatics at Helmholtz Munich (EMBL-EBI) and a co-lead author on the ADNI-1/GO/2 longitudinal plasma lipidomics study (PMC12269576). She contributed the bioinformatics and statistical modelling components, including the development of the plasma lipidome trajectory model for AD risk prediction.
