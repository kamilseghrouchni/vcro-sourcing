# Taylor W. Schmitz

## Summary

Taylor W. Schmitz is the senior and corresponding author on the ADNI Phase 1 serum lipidomics study (PMC10103184), affiliated with the Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry at the University of Western Ontario (London, Ontario, Canada), and also with Lawson Health Research Institute, Robarts Research Institute, and the Western Institute for Neuroscience. His research program investigates how serum lipids predict basal forebrain neurodegeneration in Alzheimer's disease.
