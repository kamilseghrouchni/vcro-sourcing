# Joshua A. Bell

## Summary

Joshua A. Bell is the lead PI for the life-course plasma NMR metabolomics study linking AD genetic liability to plasma metabolites in ALSPAC and UK Biobank (PMC10873397). He is affiliated with Bristol Medical School, Population Health Sciences, University of Bristol, and the MRC Integrative Epidemiology Unit. He holds a UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship (MR/W011581/1). He is the primary PI for both the ALSPAC NMR metabolomics cohort and the UK Biobank NMR metabolomics subset.
