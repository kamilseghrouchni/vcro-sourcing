# Michael W. Weiner

## Summary

Michael W. Weiner (MD) is the founding and long-serving Principal Investigator of the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI), launched in 2003 as a public-private partnership. His home institution is UCSF, though the source paper lists his affiliation as the consortium role "ADNI Principal Investigator." Per slug rules, the slug uses the home institution (ucsf) rather than the consortium name. Weiner is listed as lead PI across both the ADNI Phase 1 serum lipidomics cohort (PMC10103184) and the ADNI-1/GO/2 plasma lipidomics cohort (PMC12269576).
