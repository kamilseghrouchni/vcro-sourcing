# Rima Kaddurah-Daouk

## Summary

Rima Kaddurah-Daouk is a senior author and the lead PI for the Alzheimer's Disease Metabolomics Consortium (ADMC) at Duke University. She coordinates funding and metabolomics data generation for ADNI, including the plasma lipidomics dataset generated by Baker Heart and Diabetes Institute. Her affiliation is directly named as Duke University in PMC12269576, and ADMC grants are listed as issued "to Dr. Kaddurah-Daouk at Duke University."
