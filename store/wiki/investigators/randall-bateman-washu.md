# Randall J. Bateman

## Summary

Randall J. Bateman is the Director of DIAN-TU and Principal Investigator of DIAN-TU-001 at Washington University in St. Louis. He receives research support from NIA/NIH (U01AG042791) and the DIAN-TU pharma partners including F. Hoffmann-La Roche Ltd. Data access for DIAN-TU studies is governed by his team's published data access policy at dian.wustl.edu. He is the lead PI for both the DIAN-TU-001 gantenerumab OLE cohort and the double-blind arm.
