# Gerard Piñol-Ripoll

## Summary

Gerard Piñol-Ripoll is the lead PI for the Hospital Universitari Santa Maria Lleida AD/MCI plasma and CSF fatty acid cohort (PMC11095469). He heads the Cognitive Disorders Unit at IRBLleida Hospital Universitari Santa Maria, Lleida, Spain. He oversees the biobank request process for this cohort alongside Farida Dakterzada.
