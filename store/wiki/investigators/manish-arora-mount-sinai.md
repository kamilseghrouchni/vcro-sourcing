# Manish Arora

## Summary

Manish Arora is a senior author at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai and the senior lead for the Dartmouth ALS Biobank and CDC National ALS Biorepository hair elemental studies (PMC12444149). He is also the founder and CEO of Linus Biotechnology Inc., a Mount Sinai Health System spin-out developing commercial hair-based biomarkers. This creates a material conflict of interest for any commercial access request to the ALS hair data. De-identified data and code are available upon written request with a proposal to manish.arora@mssm.edu.
