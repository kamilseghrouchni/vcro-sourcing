# Dinesh K. Barupal

## Summary

Dinesh K. Barupal is a co-investigator for metabolomics methods on the ADNI Phase 1 serum lipidomics study, affiliated with the Department of Environmental Medicine and Public Health at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai. He is the developer of the ChemRICH chemical similarity enrichment analysis tool (R code available at https://github.com/barupal/ChemRICH), which was used to group the 348 annotated lipids into clusters for statistical testing. The ADNI lipidomics methods paper (ref 44 in PMC10103184) cited for pre-analytical protocol details was authored by Barupal and colleagues.
