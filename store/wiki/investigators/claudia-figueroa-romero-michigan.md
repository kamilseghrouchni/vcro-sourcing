# Claudia Figueroa-Romero

## Summary

Claudia Figueroa-Romero holds a lead PI role at the Department of Neurology and the NeuroNetwork for Emerging Therapies at the University of Michigan. She is a co-author on the ALS gut microbiome and plasma metabolomics study (PMC10834248) that longitudinally profiled gut microbiome changes in ALS and correlated them to the plasma metabolome.
