# Elijah W. Stommel

## Summary

Elijah W. Stommel is a senior author affiliated with Dartmouth Health, where he co-leads the Dartmouth ALS Biobank and Department of Neurology clinical collection. He is a co-investigator on the LA-ICP-MS hair elemental biodynamics study (PMC12444149) alongside senior author Manish Arora at Mount Sinai.
