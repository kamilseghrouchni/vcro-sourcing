# José Luís Cantero

## Summary

José Luís Cantero is a co-investigator affiliated with the Laboratory of Functional Neuroscience, Pablo de Olavide University, Seville, and CIBERNED. He contributed 74 amyloid-PET confirmed cognitively normal healthy controls to the Lleida AD/MCI plasma and CSF fatty acid cohort study (PMC11095469). He leads the Seville control sub-cohort collection.
