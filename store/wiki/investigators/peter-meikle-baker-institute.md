# Peter J. Meikle

## Summary

Peter J. Meikle is the senior/corresponding author at Baker Heart and Diabetes Institute (Melbourne, Australia) for the ADNI-1/GO/2 longitudinal plasma lipidomics study (PMC12269576). He leads the Baker Institute targeted LC-MS/MS lipidomics platform (Agilent 6490/6495C, dMRM, 781 lipid species, 49 lipid classes) that generated lipidomics data for both ADNI (n=4730 plasma samples) and ASPREE (n=3495). He is a co-investigator on the ASPREE lipidomics sub-study and is one of the corresponding authors for ASPREE data access.
