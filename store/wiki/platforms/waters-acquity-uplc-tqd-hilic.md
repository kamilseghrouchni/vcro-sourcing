## Overview

The Waters Acquity UPLC coupled to a TQD (triple quadrupole detector) tandem mass spectrometer, operated in HILIC (hydrophilic interaction liquid chromatography) mode, is used for targeted quantification of TMAO (trimethylamine N-oxide) and related trimethylammonium compounds. In the vCRO context it was used for the Sardinian ALS case-control cohort serum metabolomics study (PMC12907680). The TMAO panel is a validated gut microbiome metabolite readout used across ALS, cardiovascular, and neurological disease research.

**Coverage:** Targeted TMAO panel: TMAO, choline, betaine, carnitine, and related trimethylammonium metabolites (~10–20 analytes).
**Input:** 50–100 µL serum or plasma (fasted preferred).
**Validated for:** ALS (PMC12907680), cardiovascular disease, CKD.
