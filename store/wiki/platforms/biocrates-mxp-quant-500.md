## Overview

The Biocrates MXP® Quant 500 is an expanded targeted metabolomics kit covering 624 metabolites across 26 compound classes, including ceramides, acylhexosylceramides, oxidised lipids, and additional acylcarnitine subspecies not in the p180 panel. In the vCRO context it was used for the ADNI-1/GO/2 longitudinal serum metabolomics study (n=1430, 4063 samples; PMC12706616) run by Matthias Arnold and Gabi Kastenmüller at Helmholtz Munich. The MXP Quant 500 supersedes the p180 as the Biocrates flagship targeted platform.

**Metabolite coverage:** 624 metabolites including all p180 classes plus ceramides, acylhexosylceramides, diacylglycerols, oxidised lipids.
**Sample input:** 10–20 µL serum or plasma.
**Absolute quantification:** Yes, with isotope-labelled internal standards.
