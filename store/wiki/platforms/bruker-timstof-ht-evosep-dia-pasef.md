## Overview

The Bruker timsTOF HT (High Throughput) coupled to Evosep One chromatography is a next-generation clinical proteomics platform using data-independent acquisition with parallel accumulation serial fragmentation (DIA-PASEF). In the vCRO context it was used for the Target ALS Longitudinal Biofluid Core proteomics study (PMC12927225), enabling deep CSF and plasma proteomics across 528 paired samples at 5 longitudinal timepoints. The Evosep One provides Predefined Gradients (Whisper 40 SPD or similar) for high-throughput workflows.

**Coverage:** ~3,000–5,000 proteins in CSF; ~800–1,500 in plasma (depending on depletion status).
**Throughput:** 40–100 samples per day with Evosep.
**Key advantage:** Ion mobility separation (TIMS) adds a 4th dimension enabling better isomer resolution than standard DDA or DIA.
