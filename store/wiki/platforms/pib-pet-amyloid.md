# Pittsburgh Compound B PET (PiB-PET) Amyloid Imaging

## Summary

Pittsburgh Compound B (11C-PiB) PET is the primary amyloid imaging modality used in the DIAN-TU-001 studies, including the open-label extension. Annual PiB-PET was conducted at OLE baseline and each subsequent annual visit alongside CSF and blood collections. The DIAN-TU platform also uses Tau-PET and volumetric MRI for neurodegeneration surveillance. The short half-life of 11C-PiB (~20 min) requires an on-site cyclotron, limiting its use to specialised sites. In the OLE, amyloid plaque reduction of −0.71 SUVR from OLE baseline at year 3 was observed.
