# LA-ICP-MS Hair Strand Elemental Analysis (NWR-193 / Agilent 8800 Triple-Quad)

## Summary

Laser Ablation Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry (LA-ICP-MS) for hair strand analysis uses a New Wave Research NWR-193 nm ArF excimer laser connected to an Agilent Technologies 8800 triple-quadrupole ICP-MS. Single hair strands (~1.5 inches) are ablated at 0.50–0.55 J/cm² after a pre-ablation step to remove the outer layer, yielding ~400–800 sampling points per strand at approximately 2–4 hourly temporal resolution. This enables spatiotemporal biodynamic profiling of 17 elements including Cu, Zn, Cr, Ni, Mn, Se, and others. The LinusBio clinical laboratory (Arora group, Mount Sinai) uses the same method parameters for clinical-grade analysis.
