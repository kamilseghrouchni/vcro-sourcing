# Nightingale Health 1H-NMR Targeted Metabolomics Platform

## Summary

The Nightingale Health 1H-NMR targeted metabolomics platform measures 229–249 plasma or serum metabolites and ratios via proton NMR spectroscopy. It was used for both the ALSPAC life-course cohort (229 metabolites, EDTA plasma, fasted except age-8) and the UK Biobank phase-one NMR release (249 metabolites and ratios, EDTA plasma, non-fasting). The platform focuses on lipid (VLDL/LDL/HDL subfractions, phospholipids, triglycerides) and non-lipid metabolites (amino acids, glucose, lactate, citrate). Direct comparability between ALSPAC fasted and UK Biobank non-fasted timepoints is limited for triglyceride- and glucose-sensitive metabolites.
