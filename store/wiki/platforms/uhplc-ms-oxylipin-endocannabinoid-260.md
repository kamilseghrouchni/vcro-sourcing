## Overview

The UHPLC-MS/MS Targeted Oxylipin-Endocannabinoid (OxyL-EC) panel (~260 analytes) is a specialised targeted mass spectrometry method developed at the UC Davis West Coast Metabolomics Center (Borkowski group) for comprehensive profiling of lipid mediators including oxylipins, endocannabinoids, bile acids, and specialised pro-resolving mediators. In the vCRO context it was used for the ADNI 2/GO plasma oxylipin-endocannabinoid cohort (n=763, baseline cross-sectional; PMC12857118). The SCIEX QTRAP 6500+ or equivalent is the mass spectrometer of choice for this panel. Dual-pH solid-phase extraction (acidic for oxylipins, basic for endocannabinoids) enables coverage of compounds with very different physicochemical properties in a single workflow.

**Coverage:** ~260 analytes: prostaglandins, thromboxanes, leukotrienes, HETEs, EETs, endocannabinoids (AEA, 2-AG), bile acids, NAEs, SPMs.
**Input:** 0.5–1 mL plasma; dual-pH SPE required.
**Pre-analytical requirements:** Immediate processing after blood draw; −80°C storage; ≤1 freeze-thaw cycle.
