## Overview

The SCIEX 6600+ TripleTOF is a high-resolution hybrid quadrupole time-of-flight mass spectrometer. At Leiden LACDR it is used with HILIC chromatography for untargeted and targeted polar metabolomics (amino acids, nucleotides, organic acids) and oxylipin profiling of plasma and fecal samples. In the vCRO context it was used for the Geneva COSCODE cohort (PMC12572818) — the largest paired plasma+fecal metabolomics study in the MCI spectrum at the time of publication.

**Coverage:** Polar metabolites (HILIC mode) + oxylipins and lipid mediators (RPLC mode).
**Input:** Plasma or fecal extract; typically 50–100 µL plasma equivalent.
**Resolution:** ~35,000 FWHM; MS/MS for structural confirmation.
