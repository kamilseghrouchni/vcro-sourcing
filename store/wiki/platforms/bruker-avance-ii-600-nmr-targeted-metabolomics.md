# Bruker AVANCE-II 600 MHz 1H-NMR Targeted Metabolomics (Greifswald)

## Summary

The Bruker AVANCE-II 600 MHz NMR spectrometer operated by TOPSPIN 3.2 at University Medicine Greifswald was used for targeted 1H-NMR urine metabolomics in the DELCODE sub-cohort. Fifty metabolites were quantified using B.I.Quant-UR, with creatinine normalisation and Probabilistic Quotient Normalisation (PQN) as alternative approaches. The NOESYPRESAT pulse sequence with automated SampleJet delivery is used. This is a single-site instrument-specific platform, distinct from the vendor-kit Nightingale NMR approach — external replication requires a comparable Bruker 600 MHz instrument.
